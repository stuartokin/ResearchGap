Early evidence

Google DeepMind’s GNoME predicted 2.2 million new crystal structures (2023). Microsoft and PNNL used AI to screen 32 million candidates and make a new battery electrolyte (2024). AI-designed drug candidates are progressing through clinical trials. AI-generated layouts are used in Google’s TPU chips. Network digital twins are entering planning and connections work.

What AI does not compress

Factories, clinical trials, safety cases, standards, spectrum, planning consent and grid connections. That is why physical fields shrink less than software, and why regulation and assurance increasingly set the pace.

    Data status

    What the model shows

    Each arc traces one breakthrough from the first published or demonstrated research to its first commercial product, operational use or binding change. Lanes group arcs by where the research began (universities, industry labs, or government and publicly funded labs) or by field. The height of each arc is the gap in years. “Today” is the date the data was last updated, and the outlook always runs ten years beyond it.

    Definitions

    • Research is the first credible publication, patent or demonstration of the core idea.
    • Mainstream adoption is the year the technology reached widespread, routine use: roughly 10% or more of its potential market, or standard practice in its sector. Shown as a second, thinner arc. Most are AI estimates until the agents find a source.
    • Region is where the research began; “Many countries” marks work with no single origin.
    • Change is the first commercial sale, operational deployment, or rule taking effect. It is not mass adoption, which usually takes longer.
    • Verification: “Source-cited” records link to a source that was checked; “AI-compiled” records were compiled by AI from standard references; “AI estimate” marks outlook years with no published target.
    • Trend lines join the median gap for each lane in each era. Beyond today, the gap chart shows a scenario at the recent pace and with AI and digital twins, using stated assumptions.
    • Company product arcs run from founding or first prototype to first commercial release. Founder wealth uses the first listing, sale or valuation that made founders multi-millionaires; net worths carry a dated source.

    Caveats

    • Dates can be approximate and some attributions are contested; many breakthroughs drew on all three sources.
    • The sample is curated, not exhaustive, and only includes ideas that succeeded.
    • Physical and safety-critical sectors stay slower than software because of capital, safety and regulatory cycles. That is often appropriate.
