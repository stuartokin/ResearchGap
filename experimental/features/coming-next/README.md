# Coming next

**What it shows:** the outlook milestones (the ones the data expects to become real-world change, with an
AI-estimated year), grouped by that year, soonest first: any whose estimated year has already passed without them
landing, then each year from this year to ten years ahead, then everything later. Each group shows how many there are
and names every milestone. Milestones in an AI for clean energy area are marked with a purple dot and listed first.

**How to use it:** open Charts, choose the ✨ Coming next tab. Use "AI for clean energy" to show only those
milestones, and "All fields" to go back. Select a milestone to see, in the side panel, its field, the year research
began, the expected year, the expected gap from research to change, and, for comparison, the median gap of the
landed milestones in the same field that landed in the last ten years (or of all landed ones in that field in your view, if none did).
Then open its story. It follows your filters.

**Insight:** how many outlook milestones are expected by ten years from now, how many of them are in AI for clean
energy, and their median expected gap from research against the median gap of milestones that landed in the last
ten years. Every figure comes from the data. The insight always counts all fields in your view, whichever button is
selected.

**Honesty:** the expected years are AI estimates compiled from public sources, not forecasts, and may be wrong. An
expected gap is only as good as that estimate. Milestones still in the lab have no expected year and are left out.
"This year" and "ten years ahead" use the date on the visitor's device.

**Why it was added:** 11 November 2026, as the agent's own improvement (both open idea issues, #35 and #36, already
have a chart). The app is about how quickly research becomes change and what is coming; no chart listed the coming
milestones year by year, with AI for clean energy picked out.
