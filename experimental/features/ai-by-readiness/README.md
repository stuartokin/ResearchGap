# AI by readiness

**What it shows:** for clean energy technologies (those in an AI for clean energy area), how many at each stage of
readiness (research, pilot, first commercial use, at scale) are AI-driven.

**How to use it:** open Charts, choose the ✨ AI by readiness tab. Select a bar to list its AI-driven technologies in the
side panel; select one to open its story. It follows your filters.

**Insight:** compares the AI-driven share at scale with the share still in research; every figure comes from the data.

**Why it was added:** the sample feature that proves the AI-enhanced version works end to end (added by hand, not by the agent).
