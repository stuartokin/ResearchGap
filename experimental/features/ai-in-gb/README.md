# AI in Great Britain

**What it shows:** the AI-driven clean energy technologies in the data (those in an AI for clean energy area),
grouped by how close they are to use in Great Britain, closest first: in use in Great Britain, in trials, planned,
not yet, and not known. Each group shows how many there are and names every technology.

**How to use it:** open Charts, choose the ✨ AI in Great Britain tab. Select a technology to see, in the side panel,
its Great Britain status, its readiness worldwide and whether it is AI-driven; then open its story, which gives the
data's note on Great Britain and the sources. Use
"All clean energy" to include clean energy technologies that are not AI-driven, and "AI-driven only" to go back. It
follows your filters.

**Insight:** how many of the AI-driven clean energy technologies are in use in Great Britain so far, in trials and
planned. Every figure comes from the data. The insight always counts AI-driven technologies only.

**Honesty:** the Great Britain status is an AI estimate compiled from public sources and may be wrong or out of
date. A technology with no Great Britain record is counted as "Not known".

**Why it was added:** 4 November 2026, for idea issue #35 ("A view showing which AI-driven clean energy technologies
are closest to being used in Great Britain"). The app records a Great Britain status for many technologies, but no
chart showed it on its own.
